## Business-visible outcome

A visitor can open, share and print one stable vehicle page with approved details and media.

## In scope

- Create one semantic detail route, consolidating POC modal/page duplication.
- Build identity, price, availability, specifications and accessible main gallery/thumbnails.
- Apply approved visibility to auction/Japan/Durban media with safe fallbacks.
- Display only the approved public projection, including Active/Deactive and country visibility; do not expose internal source-system or unpublished media fields.
- Export reusable detail/gallery behavior through `packages/vehicle-detail`; app wrappers provide audience-specific visibility and actions.
- Render approved price versus price-request CTA and clearly labelled contact intent. Wishlist, compare and PDF remain excluded unless separately approved.

**Concrete deliverables** — where the scope above lands in this repository, so no time is spent rediscovering it:

- **Route**: `apps/public-web/src/app/vehicles/[slug]/page.tsx`, server-rendered, resolving the vehicle by the stable slug with immutable-identifier fallback delivered by `D01-P04-T001`. Do not modify `apps/public-web/src/app/vehicles/page.tsx`, which is the existing inventory search page.
- **Feature slice**: `apps/public-web/src/features/vehicle-detail/{components,hooks}` plus `vehicle-detail.css`, following the Phase 1.2–1.3 slice pattern.
- **Specifications** come from the existing `VehicleDomainSummary` fields; see the context pack §3.
- **Gallery**: accessible main image with thumbnails — keyboard operable, visible focus, correct roles and labels, no nested interactive controls, no fixed DOM `id` in a reusable component.
- **Private fields**: never render `internalNotes` or `staffOwnerId`; they exist on the mock record but not on the public projection.
