## Business-visible outcome

The mock service provides stable, customer-owned PI/invoice records and authorized printable/document projections.

## In scope

- Define minimal list/detail/print-or-download metadata operations for PI and approved commercial invoices.
- Link vehicles, payments, totals, paid/outstanding, currency, due date and document visibility.
- Preserve historical issued values and one authoritative identifier; model unavailable/restricted documents.
