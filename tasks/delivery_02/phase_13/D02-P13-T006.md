## Business-visible outcome

A signed-in customer can navigate to "My Inquiries" and see every vehicle they have requested a quote for, each showing how many people in total are inquiring about it.

## In scope

- New `apps/customer-app/src/features/inquiries/`: `CustomerInquiriesPage.tsx`, `useCustomerInquiries.ts` (calls `CustomerInquiryService.list()`), `inquiries.css`, `__tests__/inquiries.test.ts`, `index.ts`.
- Each list row shows the vehicle's title/thumbnail (reuse existing vehicle-card presentational primitives where they fit), the date the customer first requested a quote for it, and "`<N>` people are inquiring" (singular "1 person is inquiring" when `inquirerCount === 1`).
- Empty state when the customer has no inquiries, with a link back to inventory browsing.
- `apps/customer-app/src/app/App.tsx`: add `if (activeRoute === '/inquiries') { return <CustomerInquiriesPage />; }` following the existing `/inventory` branch pattern.
- `apps/customer-app/src/features/shell/CustomerShellProvider.tsx`: add `{ id: 'inquiries', label: 'My Inquiries', href: '/inquiries', icon: '💬' }` to `DEFAULT_NAV_ITEMS`.
