**Services Page Content:** Create a dedicated "Services" page (e.g., `/services`) in the public web app displaying the provided marketing copy:
- **Vehicle Shipment:** Highlight that CarChief processes cars for shipment to local agents (taking approx. two weeks, significantly faster than the normal 3-4 weeks) utilizing tie-ups with major Japanese shipping companies.
- **Additional Services:** Detail that CarChief provides insurance, inspection, and the exclusive CarChief Warranty (benefitting customers purchasing vehicles directly).
- **Clearing Services:** Emphasize the single-point-of-contact value proposition, noting that CarChief handles the entire clearing process through local agents at destination ports.
- **Border Delivery Logistics:** List the logistical partnerships for delivering vehicles to specific borders:
  - Zimbabwe: Beitbridge, Chirundu, Harare, Nakonde, Plumtree, Tunduma
  - Zambia: Chirundu, Lusaka, Ndola, Kabwe, Kitwe, Nakonde, Tunduma, Songwe, Kasumulu
