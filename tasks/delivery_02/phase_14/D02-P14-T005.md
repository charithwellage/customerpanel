## Requirements and rules

**Profile-driven Localization:** When a user logs into the Customer App, the primary vehicle feeds and searches must automatically default to displaying cars suitable for their registered country.

**Regulatory Compliance Filtering:** Different destination countries have strict import rules and regulations governing which vehicles are eligible (e.g., Kenya, South Africa, Sri Lanka, Dubai, UK, etc.).
- The system must enforce a compliance filter that evaluates vehicles against the rules of the user's logged-in country.
- Examples of country-specific rules to enforce programmatically:
  - Year of Manufacture (YOM) restrictions (e.g., Kenya allows maximum 8 years old).
  - Steering configuration (LHD vs RHD).
  - Roadworthiness/Inspection requirements (JEVIC, QISJ, etc.).
- The UI must exclusively show vehicles that are **eligible** for import to that country, or clearly flag non-compliant vehicles if the user overrides the destination search.

## Business-visible outcome

Users who log into the application will experience a personalized, compliant vehicle marketplace. Customers from Kenya will only see vehicles that meet the strict 8-year import rule and are Right-Hand Drive. This localization reduces customer frustration, minimizes order cancellations due to regulatory failures, and streamlines the sales pipeline.
