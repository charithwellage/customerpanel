## Requirements and rules

**Enhanced Status Visualization:** Customers frequently visit the "My Vehicles" (formerly Sourced Vehicles) page to track their cars. The current UI is too basic.
- Redesign the vehicle cards/list on this page to prominently feature a highly visual **Status Tracker** (e.g., a stepper or progress bar) showing exactly where the vehicle is in the fulfillment pipeline (e.g., Sourced ➔ Inspection ➔ Port ➔ Shipped).
- Ensure the status visually pops (using appropriate semantic colors: warning, success, info) so the user understands the state at a glance.

**Usability & Interaction Improvements:**
- Implement interactive elements such as expandable rows or slide-overs to view detailed status logs or next steps without navigating away from the list view.
- Add clear Calls to Action (CTAs) relevant to the current status (e.g., "Upload Documents", "View Invoice", "Track Shipment").
- Elevate the overall aesthetic quality to match premium e-commerce experiences (glassmorphism accents, smooth hover micro-animations, clear typography hierarchy).

Customers will experience a significant reduction in anxiety regarding their vehicle purchase.
