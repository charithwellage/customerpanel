## 1. Implementation Summary

Implemented the public content pages and regional branch hub routes for CarChief using a single shared presentational component, `ContentPageTemplate.tsx`.

Key features implemented:
- **Server Data Loaders (`apps/public-web/src/features/public-content/loadPublicContent.ts`)**:
  - `loadContentPages()`
  - `loadContentPageBySlug(slug)`
  - `loadOffices()`
  - `loadBranches()`
  - `loadBranchById(idOrCode)`
  - `loadBranchVehicles(branch)` using `@carchief/api-client` services.
- **Shared Presentation (`ContentPageTemplate.tsx`)**:
  - Composes hero header, title (`<h1>`), badge, metadata pills (owner, updatedAt, status), body paragraphs, sections array, hero action CTAs, loading spinner state (`role="status"`), empty state, and unavailable/error recovery state (`role="alert"`).
- **Sub-components**:
  - `ContactPageContent.tsx`: Global support details, hotline, WhatsApp link, and regional office cards with headquarters badge.
  - `BranchPageContent.tsx`: Regional hub identity, code, destination port, welcome & contact banners, team members list, Meet & Greet service info, contact details, and branch vehicle collections (**Latest Arrivals** and **Recently Discounted** via `VehicleCollectionPrimitive`).
  - `PrivacyPageContent.tsx`: Cookie consent choice toggle ("Essential Cookies Only" / "Accept All Cookies") with persistent choice state.
- **9 Next.js Page Routes**:
  1. `about/page.tsx` (`PUB-17` - About Us)
  2. `how-to-buy/page.tsx` (`PUB-18` - 5-Step Buying Guide)
  3. `contact/page.tsx` (`PUB-21` - Contact & Offices)
  4. `services/page.tsx` (`PUB-30` - Company Services)
  5. `auction/page.tsx` (`PUB-27` - Auto Auction Services & Calendar)
  6. `shipping-schedule/page.tsx` (`PUB-29` - Ocean Freight Vessel Schedule with safe recovery state)
  7. `privacy/page.tsx` (`PUB-31` - Privacy & Cookie Policy)
  8. `content/[slug]/page.tsx` (`PUB-30` - Dynamic Company Content)
  9. `branches/[branch]/page.tsx` (`PUB-35` - Dynamic Branch Hubs)
